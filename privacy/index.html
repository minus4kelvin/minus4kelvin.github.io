What applies across every product

Whatever a product’s own policy says, the same principles apply to everything {{ site.entity.name }} operates:

  • You are not required to create an account to use a product.
  • Data stays on your device unless you opt in to something that needs a server.
  • We do not sell personal data, and we do not share it with data brokers.
  • Every product publishes how to delete your data — through an in-app control, by email, or both.
  • Payment details are handled by the app store or payment processor, never by us.
  • We do not build advertising profiles from your product data.

Our own data handling

This website does not use analytics, does not set advertising cookies, and does not run any third-party scripts. It is served as static files and nothing about your visit is recorded beyond ordinary web-server logs.
