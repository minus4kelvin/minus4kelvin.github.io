Nhom

Find your people. Do the thing.

Nhom is a social app for local and online events. Browse lobbies for sports, food, gaming, nights out and dates; filter by activity, distance and time; then join or host your own.

Every lobby comes with group chat, a feed and recurring groups, so the meetup doesn’t die in the comments. Lobbies also rank and compete, turning a casual meetup into something worth showing up for. Nhóm means group in Vietnamese — and also to light or kindle.

Mines of Idle Doomath

Keep the crew mining. Just answer the maths.

A single-player idle game about a small crew working a very deep shaft. Solve a short equation to earn minerals, buy upgrades and hired miners, spend gems on a second upgrade track, and sink a new shaft when a run slows down for a permanent multiplier.

Free on every platform. No paywall on progression, no banner or interstitial ads, and every miner, upgrade and cosmetic is earnable by playing. The crew keeps digging while you are away.

How we work

Short, checkable statements rather than vague marketing copy.

🛠

Built and operated in-house

Design, engineering, backend, art, audio and writing are all done by the studio. We do not license someone else’s product and call it a portfolio.

📱

Three platforms, one codebase

Every product ships to iOS, Android and the web from a single project, so platforms feature-parity instead of arriving years apart.

🌍

Built for real communities

Nhom runs in English and Tiếng Việt, because the people it is for speak both.

Privacy by default

Both products are usable without an account, keep data locally unless you opt in, and publish a one-step deletion path.

💬

Real human support

A named person reads every message, on an address at our own domain, with support hours and a stated reply time.

🎮

Free stays free

No paywall on core progression. Purchases are cosmetic or convenience only and never outpace someone who never pays.
